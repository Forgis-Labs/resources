# The Forgis ML Sheet

Machine learning from first principles to the frontier, in six A2 sheets. It's written for
people joining the Forgis research team, and it's useful to anyone who wants the whole picture
on one wall.

107 cards, 75 figures and diagrams, and 31 questions at the end with no answers printed.

Printing the A4 versions: print at **100% or "Actual size"**, not "Fit to page", or the tiles
will not line up. Cut each tile on its trim line and tape them together.

## The sheets

| Sheet | Covers |
|---|---|
| I Foundations | what learning is, probability and information, statistics, classical ML |
| II Training a network | optimisation, activations, losses, tokenisation and patching |
| III Architectures and evaluation | the Transformer and friends, metrics and evaluation |
| IV Representation and uncertainty | representations, probabilistic modelling, synthetic data |
| V Time series | time series |
| VI Scale and the frontier | scaling, adaptation and alignment, learning without labels, questions |
